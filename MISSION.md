# Mission

> Filled in by the Commander. Every staff and intel tasking starts here. Leave a field as `✍️` until it's decided.

## Mission
*What must be true when this is over? One testable sentence, with a date.*

✍️

## Commander's intent
- **Purpose:** why this mission matters. ✍️
- **Key tasks:** the few things that must happen, however the plan changes. ✍️
- **End state:** what "done" looks like. ✍️
- **Risk:** what I will and won't risk, and what I'll spend (time, money, reputation). ✍️

## Constraints
*What the staff must do, or must not do.* ✍️

## Scope and sensitivities
- **In scope:** ✍️
- **Out of scope:** what doesn't belong in this repository at all. ✍️
- **Sensitive:** sources, people or information the staff must protect, and that tearlines must leave out. ✍️
- **Do not read or write:** other repositories, folders or accounts that are off-limits. ✍️

## Planning guidance (optional)
*The courses of action I want considered or ruled out, and any deadlines.* ✍️
